Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Add products to your cart, enter your delivery details, then tap “Send order on WhatsApp”. We will confirm stock, shipping and payment with you there.

Do you deliver across India?

Yes. Delivery time and charges depend on your PIN code and order size. We confirm both before payment.

How can I pay?

After order confirmation, we share a secure payment link or verified UPI details on WhatsApp.

Can I order gifts in bulk?

Yes. Contact us on WhatsApp for wedding, Umrah return-gift and event quantities.

What if a product arrives damaged?

Send an unboxing video and photos on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery. Please read our return policy for full details.
