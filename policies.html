Delivery & support

Shipping and Return Policy

We confirm product availability, shipping charges and the estimated dispatch timeline on WhatsApp before accepting payment. Delivery time depends on destination PIN code and courier availability.

Damaged items

Please record a continuous unboxing video when opening the parcel. If an item arrives damaged or incorrect, contact us on WhatsApp within 24 hours of delivery with the unboxing video, parcel label and clear photos. Eligible cases will be resolved through a replacement or another mutually agreed solution.

Returns and exchanges

Because several products are fragrances, personal-use goods or specially packed gifts, change-of-mind returns may not be available. Do not use, open or alter an item you want us to review. Final eligibility is confirmed on WhatsApp before return shipping.

Colours and handmade details

Screen colours, finishes and handcrafted details can vary slightly from photographs. Such small variations are not treated as defects.

Your information

Privacy Policy

We use the name, phone number, delivery address and order details you share only to process your enquiry, fulfil your order, provide support and send offers you have chosen to receive.

This static website stores cart contents in your own browser using localStorage. Cart information is not sent anywhere until you choose to open and send the prepared WhatsApp message.

We do not sell customer information. WhatsApp, UPI providers, couriers and payment-link services may process information under their own privacy policies.

Before ordering

Terms and Conditions

Website prices, offers and availability can change. An order becomes confirmed only after Haramain Collection verifies stock, total price, shipping and payment with you on WhatsApp.

Product descriptions are provided in good faith. Please ask for current measurements, kit contents, fragrance notes, colours or quantities before payment when these details are important to your purchase.

Never send payment to an unverified number or UPI ID. Use only the payment details confirmed in your active WhatsApp conversation with the store.

Owner note: These starter policies should be reviewed and updated with your final registered business name, address, tax details and professional legal advice before public launch.
