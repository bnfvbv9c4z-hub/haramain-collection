# Haramain Collection — Project Brief

## Store and audience

- **Store name:** Haramain Collection
- **Business:** Islamic products and gifts ecommerce store
- **Customers:** Men, women, families, Hajj/Umrah pilgrims, Islamic gift buyers, and fragrance lovers

## Product catalogue

- Quran boxes
- Perfumes and attar
- Complete Hajj and Umrah kits
- Empty Zamzam bottles
- Bakhoor and oud
- Oud daan / incense burners
- Islamic décor and additional related products
- **Launch catalogue:** Approximately 50–100 products
- **Expected price range:** ₹100–₹10,000

## Brand direction

- **Personality:** Premium with a traditional Islamic character
- **Palette:** Warm white `#FAF8F2`, beige `#DCCDB8`, sage green `#718C72`
- **Style:** Modern, minimal, soft and trustworthy with subtle Islamic character
- **Typography:** Elegant headings with clean and highly readable body text
- **Logo:** Supplied Arabic calligraphy medallion, prepared as a transparent website asset
- **Reference sites:** None supplied; the design should feel original
